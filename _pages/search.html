    Searches the FeynCalc manual (stable and development), the FeynHelpers manual and the example gallery, right here in your browser. Pages whose name matches come first, then the paragraphs that mention it. To jump straight to a function by name, press ⌘K (Ctrl K) or / on any page.

    Formulas are found however they are written: γ^μγ^ν, \gamma^\mu \gamma^\nu and Mathematica's \[Mu] all work. Type \ and a few letters (\gam) in the box, or click Σ, to enter a symbol.
